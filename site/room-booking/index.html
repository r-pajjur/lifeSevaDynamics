Review & Reserve

– Almost Home –

A 25% deposit reserves your room today; the balance is settled at check-out. Suggested contribution rates support the upkeep of the Ashrama.

Cancellation Policy

  • 14 or more days before arrival — full refund of your deposit.
  • 7–13 days before arrival — 50% of your deposit is refunded, or the full deposit may be applied to a future stay within 12 months.
  • Fewer than 7 days before arrival — the deposit is retained as a contribution to the Ashrama.
  • Date changes are free once, subject to availability.
  • If the Ashrama must cancel your stay for any reason, your deposit is refunded in full.
Please agree to the cancellation policy to continue.
